What the reader looks for

Any of these labels on the paper ticket will be picked up, with or without a colon: System no, System name, System id, Business units, Answer code, Priority, Status, Reported by, Issue, Date. Failing a label, it falls back to matching your system names, spotting an id that fits that system's pattern, and finding a four digit code it recognises. Anything it cannot find is flagged rather than guessed.

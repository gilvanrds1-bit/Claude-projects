Quick Start Guide

The Watch Box

Keep track of the watches you own and the ones you're dreaming of — all in one handsome box. No account, no sign-up, no cost.

1

Open it & keep it handy

Works in any web browser, on computer or phone.

  • Tap Open the Watch Box above (or paste the link into your browser).
  • Bookmark it — on a computer press Ctrl+D; on a phone use "Add to Home Screen". That's your one-tap way back in.
  • Your collection saves itself automatically on your own device. Nobody else can see it.
2

Your box at a glance

Here's what you're looking at.

  • Top row of numbers — how many you own, how many are on your wishlist, and their total value.
  • All / Collection / Wishlist — tap to switch between everything, only the ones you own, or only the ones you want.
  • Search box — find a watch by brand, model or reference number.
  • ＋ Add watch — add a new watch (next step).
  • A small bookmark on a watch's brass plate marks it as a wishlist item; watches you own have no mark.
  • Backup / Restore — your safety net (step 6 — don't skip it!).
3

Add a watch

Owned or wished-for — both live here.

  1. Click ＋ Add watch.
  2. Start typing the Brand — a list of well-known makers pops up. Pick one, or type your own.
  3. Add the Model name (the only must-have), plus any details you know: reference, movement, dial size, lug-to-lug.
  4. Choose the status: ○ Wishlist for one you want, or ● In collection for one you own. If you own it, add the purchase date and cost.
  5. Add a photo (optional): click Upload photo to pick one from your device, or paste a direct image link.
  6. Click Add to box. It appears in your box straight away.
4

Look at, edit or remove a watch

Everything about a watch, one tap away.

  • Click any watch in the box to see its full details.
  • Click Edit to change anything, then save.
  • Click Delete to remove it — it asks you to confirm first, so you can't delete by accident.
5

Filter, search & reorder

Arrange the box the way you like.

  • Filter: use the All / Collection / Wishlist tabs at the top.
  • Search: type any brand, model or reference in the search box.
  • Reorder: in the All view, click and drag a watch to a new spot — a gold line shows where it will land, and the new order is saved. (Dragging uses a mouse or trackpad.)
6

Keep your collection safe

The one habit worth having. Please read this.

  • Your watches are saved in this browser, on this device only. They are not in the cloud and not tied to an account.
  • Click ⇩ Backup to download a single file containing your whole collection. Keep it somewhere safe (another folder, a USB stick, cloud storage, or email it to yourself).
  • Moving to a new phone or computer, or starting fresh? Open the app there, click ⇧ Restore, and choose your backup file — everything comes back.
  • Good habit: click Backup whenever you've added or changed a few watches. That's all it takes to never lose anything.

Quick answers

Do I need an account or password?

No. Just open the link and start adding watches.

Will other people see my watches?

No. Each person who opens the app has their own private box on their own device. Sharing the link shares the app, never your collection.

Does it cost anything?

No — it's completely free to use.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

Yes, but each device keeps its own separate collection. To copy your watches across, use Backup on one and Restore on the other.

I cleared my browser / got a new device — where did my watches go?

They live in the browser, so clearing its data removes them. This is exactly what Backup protects against: restore your latest backup file and you're back.

Does a photo have to be uploaded?

No. You can upload a picture from your device, paste a direct image link, or add no photo at all (a watch icon shows instead).

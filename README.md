# The Watch Box

A free, private tracker for the watches you own — and the ones on your wishlist. It's a digital take on a proper wooden watch box: brass nameplates, felt slots, the lot.

**▶ Live app:** https://thewatchboxguy.github.io/
**📖 Quick Start guide:** https://thewatchboxguy.github.io/guide.html

## What it does

- Track your collection and your wishlist, with photos, specs and prices.
- See your collection value and wishlist value at a glance.
- No account, no sign-up, no ads, and your collection is never uploaded or shared.
- Your watches are saved privately in your own browser — nothing leaves your device.
- Works offline and installs to your phone's home screen (it's a PWA).
- Back up and restore your collection as a file (on phones you can save it straight to Drive / OneDrive).

## Files

- `watch-box-standalone.html` — the entire app, in one self-contained file.
- `guide.html` — the Quick Start guide.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icon-*.png` — make the app installable and able to work offline.

The live site is published from the `gh-pages` branch.
